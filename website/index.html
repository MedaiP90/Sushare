Why Sushare

Everything your table needs

No registration. No cloud account. Just open the app and start ordering with your friends.

Shared Table Sessions

The host creates a session and shares the restaurant menu with the whole group. Everyone sees the same dishes, prices, and categories.

Join via QR

Guests join in seconds by scanning the host's QR code — no Wi-Fi needed, no pairing hassle.

Personal Sub-Orders

Each person builds their own order independently. The host sees a real-time merged view ready to hand to the waiter.

Works Offline

Everything runs over Bluetooth between devices. No internet connection required at the table — perfect for restaurants with poor coverage.

Saved Orders

Frequently visit the same spot? Save your usual order and load it in one tap next time you sit down.

Privacy First

No accounts, no tracking, no data sent to external servers. Everything stays on your device.

How it works

Ready in three steps

From launch to a shared table session in under a minute.

1

Host creates a session

Choose a restaurant from your list (or add a new one), give the session a name, and the app starts broadcasting over Bluetooth.

2

Guests join instantly

Each guest scans the QR code shown by the host or enters the 8-character session code. The menu syncs to everyone's phone.

3

Order together

Everyone picks their dishes. The host reviews the merged order and sends it to the kitchen — one clean list, no confusion.

Your data stays on your device

Sushare never sends your information to any server. Your name is stored locally on your phone only. Bluetooth and camera are used exclusively to connect with your table — not to collect data about you.
